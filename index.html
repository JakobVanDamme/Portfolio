about me

Passionate about 3D since I was 16, I hold a bachelor’s degree in Interior Design and a cum laude degree in Digital Arts and Entertainment at Howest in Kortrijk, and bring a broad skill set across 3D, 2D, CAD, and programming. I enjoy turning ideas into polished, production-ready work — whether that means modeling, design, or writing the software that supports it.

I have worked on a wide range of projects, including sculptures for Plopsaland and Albert Heijn, as well as design and software development on The Claw 2.0. I also have experience collaborating on Warner Bros. IP, and I recently started working with the NGO Accessible Gaming, where I help with their design work.

I know how to run photogrammetry quickly and rework the results in ZBrush into game-ready assets. Photography is another craft I keep expanding: I own a medium format camera and focus mainly on landscape work, always looking to improve my eye and technique.

Outgoing and driven by challenges, in my free time I enjoy solving Rubik’s cubes, running, swimming, traveling, and hiking — always looking for the next thing to learn or explore.
